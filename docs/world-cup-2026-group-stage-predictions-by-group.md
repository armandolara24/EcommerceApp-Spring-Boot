# 2026 FIFA World Cup – Group Stage Predictions (By Group)

This is the same set of score predictions and reasoning as
[`world-cup-2026-group-stage-predictions.md`](./world-cup-2026-group-stage-predictions.md),
reorganized by group (A through L) instead of by date. Within each group,
matches are listed in matchday order (Matchday 1, 2, then 3).

> **Format reminder:** 48 teams are split into 12 groups of four (A–L). The
> top two teams from each group, plus the 8 best third-placed teams, advance
> to the Round of 32.

## Methodology

Each prediction blends three inputs:

- **Current form** – the FIFA Men's World Ranking published April 1, 2026
  (France #1, Spain #2, Argentina #3, England #4, Portugal #5, Brazil #6, ...).
- **Recent major tournaments** – Euro 2024 (Spain champions, England
  runners-up), Copa América 2024 (Argentina champions, Canada's surprise
  semifinal run, Panama's group-stage win over the USA), and AFCON 2025
  (Morocco crowned champions on home soil after a contentious final against
  Senegal).
- **Long-term World Cup pedigree** – titles, historic best finishes, and
  qualifying storylines (debutants like Curaçao, Cape Verde, Jordan,
  Uzbekistan, and DR Congo; returning sides like Paraguay, Czechia, Scotland,
  Sweden, Norway, and Iraq).
